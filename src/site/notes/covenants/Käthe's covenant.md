The Intellectual; covenant with Kathe 
The intellectual explains away the daemon. She takes minimal damage because she does not believe in its source. Entanglement is slow. The intellectual playstyle benefits from access to institutional goods and the ability to anticipate its logic. The hidden cost: rationalist discipline is also what the seminary is trying to produce. The break is a vertical fall that comes late and catastrophically.
