Colors: black + pink  
Archetype: Jirai-kei  
Handed: Right  
Blood type: B

Ilse had a perception. Her family home had accumulated something and Ilse could feel it. Ilse’s attention to invisible things was unsettling to others; her family called it a breakdown. She spent fourteen months in a sanatorium and she was not treated kindly there. The diagnosis was hysteria with delusional ideation.  
  
She arrives at the seminary already egregore-fluent: she knows what this building is doing.  
  
Her sanatorium file follows her. Anything she says can be met with a past that pre-discredits her. She stopped performing sanity for anyone's benefit a long time ago. 

She is the easiest covenant to form: intensity bonds fast around shared exposure to what no one else will acknowledge is real. In the nightmare-space she is the most powerful combatant: she absorbs heavy damage but converts it into volatile strength buffs, hitting hard and moving fast. In the waking world she makes little progress: her pre-discredited status means she cannot successfully navigate rules or official structures.  
  
Ilse’s Nightmare:  
The sanatorium. The daemons here are procedural: nurses, authorities, and the hidden forms behind them. The sanatorium has a specific room she almost remembers.
