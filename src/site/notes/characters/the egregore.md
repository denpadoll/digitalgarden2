The seminary's rules manage desire, grief, ambition, and attachment among girls who have nowhere to discharge these things except into each other and into the institution. 

What [[characters/Manuela\|Manuela]] encounters are not moods or metaphors. They are real entities: the school's violence and the girl’s formative traumas having crossed a threshold into something with its own agency and appetite. 

They might be the [[locations/Lehrerinnenbildungsanstalt\|Lehrerinnenbildungsanstalt]]'s dead: former students whose accumulated suffering never dispersed; or crystallizations of specific repressive mechanisms in the institution; or fragments of the current girls' interior lives that have crossed into exterior existence through collective feeling.
