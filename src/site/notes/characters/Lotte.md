Colors: black  
Archetype: the Devotional  
Handed: Left  
Blood type: O

Lotte was born into a heretical sect organized around a charismatic leader. She was identified as a natural vessel and was trained from childhood in the discipline of complete attention: how to remain present with something that demands everything. The leader was not a mystic in the clean sense and was probably a fraud; the doctrine of suffering-as-access was not separate from the forms it took. Lotte carries the devotional architecture intact. She has an enormous capacity for total devotion and little ability to evaluate its object.

Lotte walks into the seminary and feels that she has been here before. The quasi-monastic quality of the total institution is the sect's natural cousin. She moves through its social world behind glass. The sect was separatist and the epistemology is still running. She is present, observant, and removed. She notices everything and joins nothing. The heartful covenant with Lotte is the hardest to form in the game; when it forms, she has no register for halfway. 

The [[characters/the egregore\|the egregore]] is the most total devotional object she has ever encountered, and the pull toward it is present as a threshold that can be crossed at any point if the conditions accumulate. 

Lotte’s Nightmare:  
The meeting room of the sect. The daemons do not attack, they wait. Some of them have been here before. So has she. It has a hidden room that she remembers.
