The Jirai; covenant with Ilse
When the daemon first appears in the mirror, the Jirai attacks it immediately. She knows it is real. Entanglement comes fast and hard. She arrives in the collective nightmare before she has the relational knowledge to navigate it. The Jirai takes more damage than anyone else but each hit gives her a temporary and volatile strength buff 2pt - 10pt. She learns from damage and impact.
