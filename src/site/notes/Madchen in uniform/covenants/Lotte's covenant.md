The Devotional; covenant with Lotte
Lotte understands both waking and nightmare worlds as atmospheres of devotion. Lore accumulation is the richest. The cost is the most intimate: sustained exposure erodes the boundary between observer and observed. Lotte in your party is not an obvious asset. Her attacks are weak and the covenant-formation cost is high. She carries significant traps and debuffs. She is the most narratively urgent covenant investment and the most mechanically indirect one.
