Archetype: the New Arrival  
Handed: Left  
Blood type: A

Manuela grew up in a declining family manner, her mother [[Madchen in uniform/characters/Elizabeth\|Elizabeth]] died under mysterious circumstances when she was 5, too young to properly process with narrative memory. Shortly after this her father moved away to peruse work abroad “for business”, she was left in the care of her [[Madchen in uniform/characters/Aunt Tünde\|Aunt Tünde]], who's paranoia's governed the house. Certain windows couldn't be opened. Certain things weren't spoken of. Manuela absorbed these rules without question, as children do. 

Manuela's low-level psychosis arrived in those years of solitude and was never noticed or treated: the household baseline was already distorted enough that Manuela’s instability didn’t seem that strange.
