Käthe is not who she was. The girl who survived her father's house (a brilliant and monstrous man whose work she witnessed from the inside across an entire childhood) chose a new name on the other side of it. Her file describes a serious, capable young woman of good character. She has already mapped one horror's interior logic and walked out carrying the cartographer's tools.

The rationalist framework she uses to manage everything is her father's. She is running his software; she knows what happened when he ran it. She uses it because it is the best tool she has and because the alternative is no tools at all. 

In the waking world she is the most mobile character in the game; shadow compliance and institutional memory combine to give her access to spaces and information no one else can reach. She opens restricted rooms and extracts exceptions from the institution's logic by understanding that logic. In the nightmare-space she is precise and reliable: her analysis can reveal a daemon's weak points or the underlying logic of the space.  
  
Kathe’s Nightmare:  
Her father's house; beautiful in a wrong way, orderly, designed to reward engagement with its system while never producing an exit. At its center is a room she has never been able to reach.
